03

What is in the data

Top 10 problem types

Complaints per type in the full file. One complaint can have several types.

Road alone is almost 3 in 10 labels. The classes are unequal, so we judge models by macro F1, not accuracy.

Empty category by year

Share of rows with no problem type.

2026 is 100% empty. That is why we train a text model: it fills in the missing type from the message.

How long fixing takes

Finished cases, report to last update, log scale (each step is about 10 times longer). 1,159,225 cases.

Median 7.0 daysMiddle half: 1.8 to 35.5 daysAverage 39.7 days

Half are fixed within a week, but a long tail of slow cases pulls the average up to 40 days. So we report the median, not the average. 76,503 odd times (below 0 or over 365 days) are left out.

New columns

Season, month, weekday, hour (Bangkok time), latitude and longitude from GPS, fixing days.

Outliers

76,503 fixing times below 0 or above 365 days are flagged and left out of speed analysis. A quarter of complaints are fixed within 1.8 days.

Text cleaning

Links and extra spaces removed. Thai text is read as short character pieces (2–4 letters), so no Thai word splitter is needed.
