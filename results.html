What: text models

30,000 complaints, 10 problem types, 10-fold stratified cross-validation. Hover any chart to read the numbers.

Model comparison

Mean of 10 folds. The white whisker is ± 1 std.

Every model beats the baseline by far. The baseline guesses "no" for every type, so its F1 is 0.

Model profile

Five scores on one picture. Bigger shape = better model. Scale starts at 70%.

Random Forest is careful but misses a lot: best precision (91.7%), worst recall (76.1%).

ModelMacro F1 %Precision %Recall %AUC %Hamming lossTrain time

Linear SVM wins, and it is also fast. It is the best on macro F1 and the quickest of the three real models. The ± is small (0.3), so the result is stable across the 10 folds.

    05

    What works, what does not, how we fix it

    Working

    Cleaning pipeline, text models far above the baseline, a strong rain link for flooding, clear road and flooding hotspots.

    Not yet

    Flooding dropped out of the top 10 text types after cleaning; the Thai vs. English test covered 2,569 complaints only (GPU was off).

    Fix next

    Always include Flooding, translate all 30,000 complaints on GPU, add a time-based test (train 2022–24, test 2025), ROC curves, association rules, priority score, forecast and the app.
