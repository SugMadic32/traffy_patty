03

How we test

10-fold stratified CV

Train on 9 parts, test on 1, ten times. Every model uses the same folds and seed 1992 (as in Lab 4).

No leakage

Duplicates removed before splitting, and TF-IDF is fitted inside each fold only.

Metrics

Macro F1 (main, every type counts equally), precision, recall, AUC, Hamming loss; silhouette for clusters; Spearman for rain.
