03

How we test

10-fold stratified CV

Train on 9 parts, test on 1, ten times. Every model uses the same folds and seed 1992 (as in Lab 4).

No leakage, future test

Duplicates removed before splitting, TF-IDF fitted inside each fold only. Text and forecast models are also tested on 2025 data they never saw.

Metrics

Macro F1 (main, every type counts equally), precision, recall, AUC, Hamming loss; silhouette and Gi* z for hotspots; lift for rules; Spearman for rain; MAE ± std for the forecast.
