How it works and its limits

1Your text

Any of 30 languages. Long text is split into short pieces, so nothing is cut off.

2NLLB-200 in your browser

Meta's model, made smaller (8-bit), runs with transformers.js. No server.

3Our text model

TF-IDF + Linear SVM from our final notebook reads the Thai and gives the type.

Limits

  • The first download is about 900 MB. Phones with little memory may struggle; a laptop works best.
  • About 1–5 seconds per sentence.
  • Mistakes with slang, typos, mixed languages and place names. Check important text with a person.

The problem-type model

  • Knows the 11 Bangkok complaint types.
  • For other topics, its guess means little.
  • Score above 0 = yes. Further from 0 = more sure.

Use it for

  • Reading Thai complaints when you cannot read Thai.
  • Writing to the city in your own language.
  • Checking if the type a resident chose matches the text. NLLB-200 is free for study, not for selling.
