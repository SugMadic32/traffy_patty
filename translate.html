Good to know

How it works

  • Translation: NLLB-200 by Meta (600M, compressed to 8-bit), run with transformers.js. The same model family as our notebook.
  • Problem type: our own TF-IDF + Linear SVM model, exported from the final notebook. Same answers as in Python.
  • Long text is split into short pieces, so nothing is cut off.

Limits

  • The first download is large. Use Wi-Fi. Phones with little memory may struggle; a laptop works best.
  • About 1–5 seconds per sentence, depending on the device.
  • Machine translation makes mistakes with slang, typos, mixed languages and place names. Check important text with a person.
  • The problem-type model knows Bangkok complaint words. For other topics its guess means little.

Use it for

  • Reading Thai complaints when you cannot read Thai (our team's problem).
  • Letting residents write in their own language, then sending the city a Thai version.
  • Checking whether the type a resident chose matches the text.
  • NLLB-200 is free for study and research, not for selling.
