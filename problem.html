01

The problem

Traffy Fondue is a LINE account where Bangkok residents report city problems. Over one million complaints later [1], the data is hard to use.

Wrong categories

People choose the problem type themselves, so many complaints are filed in the wrong category.

Missing categories

From November 2025 the category is empty for every complaint (100% in 2026).

No clear view

The city cannot easily see where and when each problem happens most, or which areas are slow to fix (under 1 day to 269 days).

02

How a complaint travels

The system today, and where our project plugs in.

Step 1Resident reports

Sends a photo, the location and a short message on LINE, and picks a category.

Step 2Traffy Fondue

Stores the complaint with time, GPS, district and the chosen type.

Step 3City office fixes it

The office in charge works on it; the status moves to finished.

Our projectLabel from text

Read the complaint text and give the right type, even when it is wrong or empty.

Our projectWhere and when

Find hotspots on the map, and the seasons, hours and rain linked with each problem.

Our projectWhere to focus

A priority list and a monitoring app for the city.

03

Research question

Research question

Where, when and what kinds of problems do Bangkok residents report most on Traffy Fondue, and which areas and times should the city focus on, using complaint types labelled from text (including translated text)?

Sub-questionWhat we askData mining trackStatus
RQ1 WhatWhich model labels the problem type from text best? Does it still work after Thai to English translation?ClassificationDone: 91.4%, Thai 91% vs English 73%
RQ2 WhereWhich districts and spots are hotspots per problem type? Are they real or chance?Clustering (DBSCAN, HDBSCAN), Gi*Done: 450 tested hot cells
RQ3 When and whyWhich season and hour have the most complaints? Is flooding linked with heavy rain?Time patterns, correlationDone: rho 0.64, Flooding 1.50 in rainy season
RQ4 FocusWhich place + time + problem go together, and where should the city focus next week?Association rules, predictionDone: rules, priority list, forecast
04

Ajarn's feedback

Milestone 1 feedback. The data is interesting, but our team cannot read Thai. Build an app to monitor problems and fixing speed by area and type, and label problem types from text because user-chosen categories can be wrong.

What we did: text labelling became the main task, we machine-translate the Thai to English, and we added spatial and time mining that feed the monitoring app; the model also fills the 577,101 empty types.
